What it does

A player with the controls you want.

One library

Play local files or connect Navidrome and Subsonic. Match local and server copies, search everything, and download music for offline playback.

Queues that stay with you

Keep requests in Up next. Save live queues with their history and position, switch between them, or restore a recovery snapshot.

Your sound

Gapless playback, crossfade, independent speed and pitch, an equalizer, AutoEQ headphone profiles and a sleep timer.

MilkDrop, built in

Over 10,000 presets, available offline through projectM. Search the collection, lock a preset or play only your favorites.

Paired devices

Pair by QR code or a manual code. Sync playlists, favorites, ratings, listens and saved queue contents. Each device keeps its own playback position.

Keep your library in order

Edit tags and artwork, find MusicBrainz metadata, create smart playlists, and scrobble to your server, Last.fm or ListenBrainz.

Install on Arch Linux

Download the signing key and check its fingerprint:

wget -O manida.asc https://manida.d3sox.me/manida.asc
gpg --show-keys --fingerprint manida.asc

5948F17F1611CC2B995BC6121F0D542126D514AB

Import and trust the key, then add the repository:

sudo pacman-key --add manida.asc
sudo pacman-key --lsign-key 5948F17F1611CC2B995BC6121F0D542126D514AB
sudo tee -a /etc/pacman.conf >/dev/null <<'REPO'

[manida]
SigLevel = Required DatabaseRequired
Server = https://manida.d3sox.me/arch/$arch
REPO

sudo pacman -Syu manida

Why another music player?

I couldn't find one with all the features I wanted. Namida came closest, but I didn't like its license. So I started Manida: an independent app for local music and music servers, with a Material You interface.
